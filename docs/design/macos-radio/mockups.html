Discair for Mac · radio (stage 2)

Mockups for the Cupertino theme, against the deployed radio API. No server or Android change is needed for anything here. Station names, songs and times are sample data. Nothing is built.

The owner's choices (2026-11-13): A2 · B2 · C1 · D2 · E1 E5 E7. The page opens on them and marks them "chosen". The other options stay for the record and are not built. Love (E3) was not chosen, so the Love buttons drawn in A2, A3, C1 and C2 are not built. The Mac uses Android's word "Owned" where these mockups say "In library". The build steps are in docs/RADIO_PLAYBACK_PROPOSAL.md, section "Stage 2 detailed plan".

Before the mockupsWhat I would change in the macOS part of the plan

  • "One view per theme" no longer applies. Zine was removed; the app has one theme, Cupertino, in Day and Night. The plan becomes one Radio layout drawn for both modes (switch below).
  • There is no menu bar item today. The proposal says "integration with the menu bar item", but the app has none (only the mini player, the widget and media keys). Building one is a new piece: decision D.
  • The sleep timer is listed as out of scope in the macOS contract (line 9). It has to be lifted together with the radio line when stage 2 starts.
  • A live stream needs its own playback path. The Mac engine plays files through two player nodes (gapless, crossfade, waveform, sample-rate matching). An endless stream fits none of that, so radio gets a separate live player behind the same player model, as Android did with a "live item": no seek, no waveform, no crossfade, no scrobble by the client, not saved in the queue. I would leave the equalizer off for radio in the first version and say so in the UI.
  • The "Mailroom tape" does not exist as a screen. Home already lists heard songs with Get (it reads /api/radio/plays). So that bullet becomes: Home's heard rows and the new Heard list show Downloaded and Owned.
  • Media keys. Play/pause stops and restarts the stream (a live stream cannot pause and keep its place). Next and Previous move through favourite stations. The seek command is switched off while a station plays.
  • Request budget (the plan did not say): the playing station's /now every 5 s only while it plays; /api/radio/now every 15 s only while the Radio page is on screen; the stations list on opening the page and after a change; nothing while the window is hidden and nothing plays.
  • Mac-only additions worth having: number shortcuts for favourite stations, "Tune to…" in the ⌘K palette, drag an image onto a station to set its logo, love, and the widget showing the station. They are in section E to pick from.
