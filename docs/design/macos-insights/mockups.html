What to look at
  • One generic card renders every lens kind. Kind-specific parts are optional extras: a progress strip when owned and total exist, dashed runs for missing_runs, a year badge for anniversaries. Unknown kinds fall back to title, body and Why.
  • Three honest states per lens: cards, "on but nothing to show" (an empty state, never made-up cards), and "off" (the server has not enabled it; today that is the real situation for all of them).
  • Every card has a text Why (the server's reasons as label/value rows) and Dismiss with Undo. Hidden canon and Blind spot cards reuse the existing Get and Save actions; there is no "you should own" wording anywhere.
  • Dismiss and Accept have no server route yet (they need owner approval), so in the real build those buttons stay hidden until the route exists. Here Dismiss works so the flow can be judged.
