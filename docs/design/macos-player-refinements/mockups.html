Lyric ticker. Shown only when the song has synced lyrics (a time-stamped lyric file). It replaces the second line (artist — album) so the bar keeps its height; the current line slides up and the old one leaves, 0.45 s, no animation under Reduce Motion (the line just swaps). Plain lyrics without time stamps never show. Setting: Settings › Player › Show lyrics in the player bar, default on, also a View menu item. Clicking the line opens Now Playing on the full lyrics. Instrumental parts and gaps show the artist — album line again.

Artwork blur. The same BlurredArtwork used on the album and artist pages (cover enlarged 1.2×, blur 24, saturation 1.3) with a 22% black layer so white text keeps its contrast. It replaces the flat tint; while the cover loads or is missing, the tint is the fallback. Contrast check needed on bright covers; the dim layer rises to 35% when the cover is bright, as the design notes already say for chips over artwork.

Sound graph. The app already computes 1000 peaks per song (the seek-bar waveform, read from the SMB mount), so no new data. Three ways to lay it behind the bar: A Mirrored centred on the bar's middle line, played part brighter; B Bars from floor like a skyline, the most legible against text; C Envelope line a thin smooth outline, least busy. All are drawn at 16–38% white so text stays readable; the 4 px progress line stays. My pick is A for the look and C as the quiet option; the choice could be a setting. When peaks are missing (not on the mount, or over budget) the bar is plain, as the seek bar is today. Under Reduce Motion nothing animates anyway: the graph is static and only the played part grows.
