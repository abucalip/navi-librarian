Paths sheet — five redesigns

Android (Compose, Material 3, Night theme). Every concept answers the same rule: open an artist or album from Paths, press Back or swipe back, and you land on Paths again, with the same kind, scroll position and open step, never on the artist the sheet belongs to. Sample data only. The dashed red outline marks the tap that leaves Paths.

The Back behaviour, once, for all five. Today the sheet calls onDismiss() and then navigates, so the artist page is the next thing on the back stack. The fix is the same under every look: Paths becomes a destination in the navigation stack (PathsRoute(mbid, name, kind)) or, if you keep a true sheet, the sheet is hosted above the artist page and hidden while another page is on top of it. Selected kind, the loaded answer, scroll offset and the open step live in the view model keyed to that route, so Back restores them without a refetch. System back, the predictive-back swipe and the in-app Back button all pop one entry. The sheet's own close (X, drag down, scrim tap) is the only thing that returns to the artist.
