Paths — five new redesigns

Android, Compose, Material 3, Night theme. Sample data from Herbie Hancock. Every concept ends with the same test: open an artist or album from Paths, press Back or swipe back, and you are in Paths again, same kind, same scroll position, same open step. The dashed red outline marks the tap that leaves Paths. Touch targets are drawn at 42px, about 50dp on a real phone.

The Back behaviour, for all five. Today ArtistPaths.kt closes the sheet (onDismiss()) and then navigates, so the artist page is the next entry on the back stack. Fix, in one sentence per look: A needs no fix because Paths is part of the artist page itself; B–E host Paths as a destination in the NavHost (a bottom-sheet or full-height destination with a route like PathsRoute(mbid, name, kind)) so the album or artist opens on top of it and Back pops to it. Kind, loaded answer, scroll offset, open step and visited steps live in a view model scoped to that route, so Back restores them without a new request. System back, the swipe-back gesture and the in-app Back button each pop exactly one entry. Only the sheet's own close (X, drag down, tap outside) goes to the artist.
