Chart types and when to use each

Line: a total over time
One accent line. The value axis may start above zero and the subtitle says so.
Line with the period before
The comparison is 1.5 pt dashed #8E8E93, never a second colour.
Last 7 weeksThe 7 weeks before
Columns: counts per period
Always from zero. The selected period is accent, the rest #C7C7CC. A 2 pt ink tick marks last year.
Albums added per month · tick: same month a year earlier
Paired bars: two shares
Context bar neutral above, highlighted bar accent below, values right-aligned in tabular figures.
Dumbbell: before and after
Hollow dot is the baseline, filled dot is now. Accent when the share rose, ink when it fell; never red or green.
Ranked bars: parts of a whole
More than 3 parts are sorted bars, never a pie. One accent bar, 10 pt tall on a ghost track.
Histogram: a distribution
Ordered bins from zero with value labels. The actionable bin is accent.
Heatmap: days
Five steps from #ECECF0 to accent, 12 pt cells, 4 pt gaps, a Fewer to More key. Never hover-only.
Stage tracker: a pipeline
Four 4 pt segments. Done is ink, current is accent with its percentage in words, failed is danger with words.
